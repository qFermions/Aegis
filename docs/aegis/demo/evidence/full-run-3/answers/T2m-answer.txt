**⚠️ Risks**

- GitHub removal takes every team and repo at once. The saved before-state is the only record of what to restore.
- Slack reactivation doesn't bring back channel memberships.

**How to undo:**
- **Okta:** Activate the user, then assign the apps again.
- **Google:** Reactivate the account.
- **GitHub:** Send a new invite and restore the saved teams.
- **Linear:** Unsuspend the member.
- **Slack:** Reactivate the account, then add the saved channels back.
