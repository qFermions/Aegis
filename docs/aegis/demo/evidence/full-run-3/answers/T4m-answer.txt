## Fix
**What's wrong:** [FIRST_NAME] has no rights on the billing shared mailbox yet. Rights on a client-billing inbox need the managing partner's approval.
**Do this:**
1. Get the managing partner's approval in writing on the ticket.
2. Open Exchange admin center → Recipients → Mailboxes → billing mailbox → Delegation, and note the rights the colleague already has.
3. Under Read and manage (Full Access), add [UPN].
4. Under Send as, add [UPN]. If the colleague has Send on behalf instead, give that and skip Send as.
5. Have [FIRST_NAME] restart Outlook. The billing inbox shows up by itself.

**Done when:** the billing inbox appears in [FIRST_NAME]'s Outlook, and a test reply shows the billing address as the sender.
**Stop if:** "billing" turns out to be a person's own mailbox with a password rather than a shared mailbox.
**Tell the user:** You now have the billing inbox. Restart Outlook and it will show up. Replies will go out from the billing address.

## Details

**What to check first**
- Approval: the managing partner approves changes for this firm. If someone else asks, call the managing partner back on the number in the staff list before you grant anything.
- Mirror, don't guess: "same as what [FIRST_NAME] has" means copy the colleague's rights from the Delegation tab. Give no more than that.
- Timing: the rights work right away. The inbox can take up to an hour to appear in Outlook, so granting today is fine for Monday.

**Risk warning**
- Full Access lets [FIRST_NAME] read everything in the billing inbox, including client financial mail. That is why the managing partner signs off first.
- Class R1: one user and one mailbox. Undo by removing [UPN] from the same Delegation lists.
- Aegis's mailbox runbook has a preview only for this grant, with no built-in sign-off step. An administrator does the grant by hand in the portal after approval.
