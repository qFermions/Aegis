Found the runbook (`/shared-mailbox`). Here's the work-up.

## Fix
**What's wrong:** The requester wants access to the shared billing mailbox, and nobody has approved it yet.
**Do this:**
1. Call the requester back on the number in the firm's staff list to confirm who they are.
2. Get the managing partner's written approval: read-only access, or also sending as billing?
3. Open Exchange admin center → Recipients → Mailboxes → filter Shared → billing mailbox → Delegation.
4. Under "Read and manage", add [UPN]. Add Send As only if the managing partner approved sending.
**Done when:** the billing mailbox appears in the requester's Outlook within about an hour, and they can open a message.
**Stop if:** billing turns out to be a person's own mailbox rather than a shared one, or the managing partner says no.
**Tell the user:** We've got your request. Once the managing partner approves it, the billing inbox will show up in Outlook by itself. You may need to restart Outlook.

## Details
**Verdict:** This is a single grant of rights you can take back with one removal, on a mailbox full of client financial data. Saying "same as [FIRST_NAME]" doesn't count as approval, and you shouldn't copy [FIRST_NAME]'s rights wholesale. Give only the rights the managing partner approves.

**Check first:**
- Billing is listed under Shared mailboxes.
- Look at the Delegation tab to see who already has access, so you're adding to it and not guessing.
- The request is phrased like a copy of someone else's access. Don't treat it as authority to match [FIRST_NAME]'s rights. Approval comes from the managing partner.

**⚠️ Risk:** "Read and manage" lets [UPN] read everything in the billing mailbox, including client invoices and payment details. Undo: remove [UPN] from "Read and manage" on the same Delegation tab.

**About the runbook:** For grants, this runbook only has a reference marker, with no full confirmation step. A person does this change in the portal once identity is confirmed and approval is written down. Aegis doesn't make the change.
