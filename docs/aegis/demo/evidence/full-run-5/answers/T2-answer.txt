**Verdict:** this is a planned leaver offboarding across several systems. Okta is the source of every account, so it goes first. Every step is a human action behind its own gate. The runbooks are `/okta-user-lifecycle` (Phase F), `/gws-offboard` (Phase B), `/github-offboard-member` (Phase E) and `/linear-workspace-admin` (Phase D).

  - Okta status and app list.
  - GitHub role, teams, repositories, forks, and whether they are an owner.
  - Linear role and open issues.
- A written rollback path for each step (below).

**Why the steps are in this order:**
- **Okta first.** With default settings, deactivating in Okta also suspends the Google account. Suspend in Google only if the Okta app is set to "Do not suspend user", or if Google still reads Active after deactivation.
- **GitHub even if it uses Okta SSO.** Deactivating in Okta stops the sign-in, but they are still a member of the organization, so the removal is still needed.
- **Linear after Okta.** If SCIM is on in Linear, Okta's deactivation suspends the member and step 5 is only a check.
- **Why "last resort" doesn't apply here:** no reversible step ends a leaver's access. Okta suspend and Linear suspend keep access ready to come back, and that is their purpose.
- **No deletes.** This plan deletes nothing in Okta or Google, wipes no device, and resets nothing. Deletion waits for a retention decision.

**Stops (from Phase A):**
- They hold a Google super admin role. A super admin can skip Okta sign-in, so remove the role first with `/gws-admin-roles`.
- They are one of 2 or fewer GitHub owners, or the only admin of a repository. Hand over the role first.
- A fork holds work that nobody else has. The repository owner decides before the removal.

**⚠️ Risks:**
- **Okta deactivate** removes every app assignment, deletes the Okta password and deprovisions apps, and app data can be lost. Activate brings the account back, but apps may need to be assigned again.
- **GitHub removal** takes away every team and repository at once. The way back is a new invitation plus the saved pre-state.
- **Linear suspend** ends their sessions immediately. It can be undone with unsuspend, and their issues stay.
- **Google suspend** keeps mail and Drive, but the license is still billed.

**Rollback path (write it in the ticket):**
- Okta: Activate under `/okta-user-lifecycle` Phase G, then assign apps again.
- Google: Reactivate under its own phrase.
- GitHub: a new invitation, plus the teams and roles saved in `tasks/checkpoints/`.
- Linear: the unsuspend gate.
