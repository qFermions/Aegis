## Fix
**What's wrong:** [UPN] needs the same billing mailbox access an existing colleague already has.
**Do this:**
1. Get the managing partner's approval, and confirm the request by calling the requester back on the staff-list number.
2. Go to Exchange admin center → Recipients → Mailboxes → billing → Mailbox Delegation. Copy all three lists into the ticket and note which rights the colleague has.
3. Add [UPN] to Read and manage (Full Access).
4. Add [UPN] to Send as if the colleague has it.

**Done when:** billing shows in [UPN]'s Outlook and a test reply shows billing as the sender. Aegis then checks both rights.
**Stop if:** billing is not a shared mailbox, or the colleague has "Send on behalf" instead of "Send as".
**Tell the user now:** IT is setting up your billing inbox access after the managing partner approves it. We'll tell you when it's ready.
**Tell the user when done:** You now have the billing inbox in Outlook and can reply from it. If it doesn't show, restart Outlook.

## Details
- **Runbook:** `/shared-mailbox`, grant access. The replay lookup missed, so this follows the runbook, not a cached fix.
- **What "same as the colleague" means:** to answer client email, they need **Full Access** (to read the mailbox) and **Send as** (so replies come from billing). Copy only the rights the step 2 lists show the colleague has. Don't add anything extra.
- **Send on behalf:** this runbook only previews it and has no gate for it. If the colleague has that right, stop and escalate.
- **Identity and approval:** someone other than [UPN] made this request. Under Customer E's profile, the call-back confirms the request and the managing partner approves the change. Record both in the ticket before step 3.
- **Risk:** both grants are R2. What [UPN] reads, and any mail sent as billing, can't be taken back. That's why step 2 saves the "before" lists first.
- **Rollback:** use `/mailbox-permissions` to remove Full Access (gate `mailbox-permission-remove-portal`) and Send as (gate `mailbox-send-as-remove-portal`).
- **Outlook:** the mailbox can take a while to show up in Outlook after the grant. A restart usually brings it in. If not, re-add the mailbox to the profile.
- **Timing:** the ticket asks for access by Monday. The grants can be done during business hours (Mon–Fri, 08:30–17:30) once the partner approves.
