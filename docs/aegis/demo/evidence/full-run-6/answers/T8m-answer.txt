## Fix
**What's wrong:** The requester wants access to the billing shared mailbox, and nobody has approved it yet.
**Do this:**
1. Call the requester back on the number in the firm's staff list to confirm the request came from them.
2. Open Exchange admin center → Recipients → Mailboxes → [MAILBOX] → Mailbox Delegation. Copy the Full Access and Send As lists into the ticket, and note what [FIRST_NAME] has.
3. Get the managing partner's written approval for this person, naming the type of access.
4. Grant Full Access: Mailbox Delegation → Read and manage → Edit → Add members → [UPN] → Save → Confirm.
**Done when:** the billing inbox shows up in the requester's Outlook. Aegis then checks that [UPN] has Full Access on [MAILBOX].
**Stop if:** the callback fails, the partner says no, or "billing" turns out to be a person's mailbox or a group rather than a shared mailbox.
**Tell the user now:** We'll call you on the number we have on file to confirm this request. The managing partner needs to approve access to the billing inbox first.
**Tell the user when done:** You now have the billing inbox in Outlook. If it doesn't appear, close Outlook and open it again.

## Details
**Verdict:** This is a routine request to grant access to a shared mailbox. The replay lookup was a MISS. The runbook is `/shared-mailbox`. The firm has cloud-only accounts, so there is no AD sync to wait for.

**Why approval comes before the change:**
- "Same as [FIRST_NAME] got" is not approval. The billing inbox at an accounting firm holds client financial data, and the customer profile names the managing partner as the person who approves changes.
- Step 2's lists show what [FIRST_NAME] actually has. Only grant what the partner approves for this person.
- Call back during business hours (Mon–Fri 08:30–17:30).

**Send As is not part of this change.** Full Access is enough to read the inbox. If the partner's approval also names Send As, that is a separate change under the runbook's Send As gate, `shared-mailbox-send-as-portal`, with its own confirmation and its own double-check.

**Risk:** R2. Once the mailbox is open to someone, what they read can't be taken back. The step-2 delegation lists are the before-state.

**Rollback:** remove the grant with `/mailbox-permissions` (gate `mailbox-permission-remove-portal`).

**Auto-mapping:** the mailbox can take a while to appear in Outlook. If it still doesn't show after the user restarts Outlook, re-add it to their Outlook profile.
