## Fix
**What's wrong:** Everyone reports slow internet, but we don't yet know if it's the office line or an online service. Remote staff are the test that tells them apart.

**Do this:** (No runbook covers this studio's network equipment, so these steps are reasoning, not a known fix.)
1. Ask one office person and one remote person whether it's slow for them. Office only points to the office line. Both points to an online service.
2. If both are slow, check the Google Workspace Status Dashboard and the Slack status page for any reported problems.
3. If it's office only, plug a Mac into the router by cable and run fast.com. Compare the result to the speed the studio pays for.
4. If the cable speed is well below that, check the internet provider's status page and open a ticket with them.

**Done when:** a speed test at the office shows close to the paid speed, and people say pages load normally.

**Stop if:** only one Mac is slow. That's a device problem, not the network.

**Tell the user now:** IT is checking whether the slowness is in the office or online. Could you tell us if you're in the office or working remotely?

**Tell the user when done:** Speeds are back to normal. Let us know if anything feels slow again.

See Details for scope and the ticket note.
