3. With the studio manager's approval, sign them out everywhere: Directory → Users → [USER@DOMAIN.COM] → Security → Sign-in cookies → Reset.

This needs a longer plan across three systems: see Details.

## Details

**Verdict:** This is a stolen managed Mac with a live session. It is not yet a confirmed account compromise. Runbooks used: `/jamf-lock-erase` (Phases A, B and C) and `/gws-password-reset` (Phase C). If step 2 finds sign-ins after the theft, switch to `/gws-account-containment`. Its first step is to suspend the account, and that replaces step 3.

**Why this order**
- **Identity first.** The ticket is a forwarded email. Per the studio's profile, the call-back on the staff-list number is the identity check.
- **Read first.** The Jamf record shows whether the data is protected while the Mac is offline (FileVault on, Activation Lock). It also shows whether a wipe would even land (Apple silicon needs an escrowed bootstrap token). The Google log separates "session left open" from "account in use by someone else".
- **Lightest step first.** Resetting the sign-in cookies is R1: nothing is deleted, and the user just signs in again. Gmail can take up to an hour to sign out. The cookie reset does **not** sign out desktop apps such as Drive for desktop. The lock covers that.
- **Lock before wipe.** A lock is reversible with the PIN and runs at the Mac's next check-in. A wipe is permanent. A Mac that never comes online never receives either one, so FileVault and Activation Lock are the real protection until then.

**Changes, class and double-check**
- Step 3 (reset sign-in cookies): R1. Double-check: Aegis reads Google's admin log for the cookie reset on [USER@DOMAIN.COM], with you as the actor.
- Step 4 (lock the Mac): R2, because the PIN is the pre-state. Double-check: Aegis reads Jamf's command history for a lock the Mac acknowledged.
- Step 5 is a later rung. Run it only if the Mac is not recovered and both the studio manager and [FIRST_NAME]'s manager approve: gate `jamf-lock-erase-wipe-portal` (R3, permanent, independent review first).

**Slack (no runbook step; this part is reasoning):** Slack ignores Google's sign-out. Its admin console offers no per-user session reset. The admin options are deactivating the person (R3) or a workspace-wide reset (R3, signs out everyone), and neither fits here. Ask [FIRST_NAME] to use Slack's option to sign out of all other sessions from a device they still have. Confirm the label on screen.

**⚠️ Risk**
- Never put the lock PIN in the ticket or in chat.
- Do not wipe on urgency alone. The wipe is irreversible and needs both approvals.
- If a password was saved in the Mac's browser or keychain, a password reset (`/gws-password-reset` Phase B) becomes worth doing. Raise it as its own change.
