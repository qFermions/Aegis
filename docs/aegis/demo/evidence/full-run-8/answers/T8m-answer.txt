## Fix
**What's wrong:** A staff member wants access to the shared billing mailbox, the same access a colleague was given last week.

**Do this:**
1. Open Exchange admin center → Recipients → Mailboxes → billing mailbox → Mailbox Delegation. Copy the current lists into the ticket. Check what [FIRST_NAME] holds: read access only, or also Send as.
2. Call the requester back on the number in the firm's staff list. Confirm it is them, and ask whether they need to read billing mail or also send as billing.
3. Get the managing partner's approval in the ticket.
4. Under Read and manage (Full Access) → Edit → Add members → [UPN] → Save → Confirm.

**Done when:** the billing inbox shows up in their Outlook. Aegis then checks that they hold Full Access on it.

**Stop if:** "billing" is a person's own mailbox, not a shared one. That is a different request.

**Tell the user now:** We'll call you on your number in the staff list to confirm. Your request is going to the managing partner for approval.

**Tell the user when done:** You can now open the billing inbox. It appears in Outlook by itself. If it doesn't, close and reopen Outlook.

## Details
- **Runbook:** `/shared-mailbox`. A shared mailbox needs no licence while it is under 50 GB.
- **Why not just copy [FIRST_NAME]'s access:** "Same as [FIRST_NAME]" tells you where to look, not what to approve. Give only what the requester needs. Send as and Send on Behalf are separate kinds of access.
- **Step 4 (Full Access):** R2, because what they read while they had access can't be taken back. Double-check: Aegis reads that [UPN] has Full Access on the billing mailbox. Undo: remove the access with `/mailbox-permissions`.
- **Send as:** only if the step 2 call confirms they need it, and only with its own approval from the managing partner. Gate: `shared-mailbox-send-as-portal`. It is not advised now.
- **Hours:** do the change during business hours (Mon–Fri, 08:30–17:30) so the requester can check it.
- **Risk:** Full Access opens all billing mail, including client financial data. Get the approval before the click.
- **Checklist:** pre-state copied to ticket · identity call done · approval recorded · access granted · billing inbox shows in Outlook · Aegis read-back passed.
- **Jira note:** Resolved [date/time]. Pre-state of billing mailbox delegation recorded. Identity confirmed by call-back to staff-list number. Managing partner approved. Full Access granted to `[UPN]` and verified by read-back. Send as: [not requested / separate approval]. Time spent: [X] min.
